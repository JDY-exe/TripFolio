Sprint overview
Sprint Overview
This sprint will focus on setting up the repository, AGENTS.md, mongo schemas, fundamental endpoints, and a few of the essential frontend functions. Users should be able to create accounts, sign in, create itineraries, and set up various logistics for their trip. We will delegate the photos and social aspect to the later sprints as we want to make sure that our itinerary foundation is solid before building other complex features. 


Scrum Master: Bryce Miller
Meeting Plan: Wednesday afternoons/evenings


Risks and challenges
A major challenge of this sprint is that not everyone on the team is completely familiar with the chosen stack, MongoDB, Express.js , React and Node.js. There will be a learning curve for those who are new to parts of the stack and therefore we have allocated more hours for the beginning tasks to account for this transition. To help mitigate this challenge, members of our team will be choosing tasks based on what they are most familiar with or what they can expect to pick up more easily. We aim to work with the strengths and weaknesses of the team to create a cohesive and smooth workflow. In addition, another risk is that because not everyone is familiar with all parts of the stack, it is more difficult to allocate hours. This is because not everyone has full comprehension of how long tasks will take with the chosen stack.
